An independent project, not affiliated with the City of New Orleans. Every page links to the City dataset it uses. Source code: GitHub.

Notes on the data

General

  • Supporting data. Every page also uses the City's Neighborhood Statistical Areas (boundaries) and Centerline (the street map). Rates per resident or per home use Neighborhood Statistical Areas Demographics 2020, the City's table of 2020 Census population and housing units. The calls page also uses Police Districts. Each chart's Download CSV opens the dataset it's about on data.nola.gov.
  • Population. Resident counts come from the 2020 Census, the most recent official count. The site will keep using them until the 2030 Census results are released, so population data is slightly out of date.
  • Every neighborhood is counted. Maps open on the populated city, so Lake Catherine and Viavant–Venetian Isles (under 100 residents per square mile) start out of view: zoom out or pick them from the menu.
  • Maps show a dot for each record that has a location inside a neighborhood. Records at the very same spot share one dot; its tooltip says how many more are there. The Heatmap checkbox shades each neighborhood by how many of the dots shown are in it; picking a neighborhood zooms to it but leaves the others shaded for comparison.
  • Shaded maps use up to seven shades with about the same number of neighborhoods in each (fewer when many neighborhoods share a value, such as zero); the key shows where each shade starts, rounded to a round number where that moves no more than one neighborhood into another shade.
  • Neighborhood figures are totals unless the key says per 1,000 residents or per 100 or 1,000 homes.

City Planning Cases

  • Cases with several types are grouped under the most consequential one. Topics come from keywords in case descriptions. A neighborhood notice (NPP) and the case it leads to are separate records; NPP notices start in 2016, which raises yearly totals from then on. Rows the City's file repeats exactly are counted once. Hearing dates are the next meeting dates the City lists; a few fall on weekends or holidays and are flagged as listed. Agenda descriptions leave out the applicant's name.

311 Requests

  • The page starts with February 2019: the City's file dates about 313,000 older requests to January 1–2, 2019, when they were moved into its current system. Request types are the City's own; types it no longer uses are counted as Other. A request is "open" while its status is Pending, and "closed" when the City closed it in its system, which for some types means the work was assigned to a contractor or referred to another department, not that it was finished. Time to close counts requests closed in the past 12 months that have a close date; most closed abandoned-vehicle requests have none. Locations are where each request was reported; about 2% have none. The map has a dot for every request filed in the past 12 months and every request still open, however old; the City's file has no addresses, so a dot's tooltip gives its neighborhood. Requests can be about any place, so rates per 1,000 residents run high where few people live, like the CBD; neighborhoods with fewer than 1,000 residents get no rate.

Short-Term Rentals (STRs)

  • The map shows active licenses for commercial, non-commercial and exempt lodging rentals. Operator permits, which license the person running a rental rather than a place, are not mapped. "Per 100 homes" divides licenses by 2020 Census housing units; neighborhoods with fewer than 500 are left out. The City's license types changed with its rules in 2017, late 2019 and 2023, so the applications chart groups them into commercial, residential (accessory, residential owner and non-commercial), temporary and other. Outcomes are each application's current status; "since expired" means the license was issued and has expired. A few applications have no date and are left off the chart.

Blight/Code-enforcement

  • Yearly charts start in 2016: the City's records date about 76,000 cases and 59,000 hearings to 2009, when older records were moved into its system. Open cases are shown by the stage the City lists and by time since the case was filed. "New cases" are grouped by the result of the first inspection. Overgrown-lot cases are the City's grass-cutting (lot abatement) cases, which start in earnest in 2021. The abatement records show no property entering abatement since 2019, so the map shows only properties still in abatement; how closed abatement cases ended is counted for the whole city. Maps count cases with a location (all but about 1%), so their totals run slightly below the charts'. The map's dots are every open case, every case filed in the past 12 months, grass-cutting cases open or filed in the past 12 months, and properties in abatement; the abatement records the site reads carry no dates. Each dot's tooltip gives the property's address as the City lists it. "Per 1,000 homes" divides by 2020 Census housing units; neighborhoods with fewer than 500 are left out.

Permits

  • Counts are permits issued, by the date the City issued them. The City's permit codes are grouped into nine types; interior demolition counts as renovation, and City demolitions count as demolitions. Every permit issued in the past 12 months is a dot at its address; a dot's tooltip gives the address and the City's own permit type. Days to permit run from the application date to the issue date. Construction values are left out because some are clearly entry errors. "Per 1,000 homes" divides by 2020 Census housing units; neighborhoods with fewer than 500 are left out.

Trees

  • The City's tree inventory as last updated on April 17, 2025, exported once and not refreshed daily.
  • Tree requests are 311 requests of the type "Tree Service", refreshed daily with the 311 data; requests without a location aren't mapped. "Open" means still listed as pending; days to close run from filing to the City's close date. "Dead or dying tree" became a request reason in April 2026.

New Businesses

  • "New applications" are occupational license applications, counted by the date filed; that dataset starts in 2018. Sectors are the City's NAICS categories; many recent applications don't have one yet ("Not stated"). Outcomes are each application's current status: "approved or issued" includes licenses that have since expired, and some older applications are still listed as filed or in review. "Licensed now" counts businesses holding a license today, with sectors matched from their business type; event and street vendors are grouped separately. Licenses with no location in the city, like many vendors and taxis, are not mapped. On the map each application since 2018, and each business licensed now, is a dot; "licensed since" is the business start date the City lists, and a few list none. Tooltips give each business's address, and for businesses licensed now its name, except where the business may be home-based: applications marked home-based, and current licenses of a home-based license type, at an apartment, or at an address where a home-based business has applied, show neither. Some addresses, such as office buildings and mailing addresses, hold hundreds of licenses, so their dots sit on one spot.

Calls for service

  • "Started by officers" means self-initiated. Arrival time runs from call creation to the first officer's arrival, for calls from the public only. The current month is left off the monthly chart until it's complete. NOPD withholds locations for medical and mental-health calls. The neighborhood map can show calls per 1,000 residents, using 2020 Census population. Counts include calls the City closed as duplicate or void (about 6%). NOPD cautions against comparing its call data over time, since classifications and systems change.

Drone flights

  • Takeoff times appear to be UTC and are shown in Central time. Purposes are grouped from a free-text field; when it's blank, the activity summary is used. Locations are takeoff points, not flight paths; flights from the same spot share one dot.

Pages not in the menu

The police calls for service and drone flights pages are off the menu for now. They are still updated daily, and links to them still work.
