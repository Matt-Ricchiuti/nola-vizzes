An independent project, not affiliated with the City of New Orleans. Every page links to the City dataset it uses. The street map under every map is the City's Centerline dataset. Source code: GitHub.

Notes on the data

  • Calls for service. "Started by officers" means self-initiated. Arrival time runs from call creation to the first officer's arrival, for calls from the public only. The current month is left off the monthly chart until it's complete. NOPD withholds locations for medical and mental-health calls. The neighborhood map can show calls per 1,000 residents, using 2020 Census population.
  • Drone flights. Takeoff times appear to be UTC and are shown in Central time. Purposes are grouped from a free-text field; when it's blank, the activity summary is used. Locations are takeoff points, not flight paths.
  • Planning cases. Cases with several types are grouped under the most consequential one. Topics come from keywords in case descriptions. A neighborhood notice (NPP) and the case it leads to are separate records.
  • Street trees. A one-time inventory export from October 2026, not refreshed daily.
  • Maps leave out neighborhoods with under 100 residents per square mile (Lake Catherine and Viavant–Venetian Isles), where there is too little data to show.
  • Other neighborhood figures are totals, not per resident.
