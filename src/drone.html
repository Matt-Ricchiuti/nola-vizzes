NOPD drone flights in 2026: a long year of parades, then a sudden shift to daily dispatch flights

For most of the year, police drones mostly flew over big events. Since mid-October, new drones have been launching from fixed downtown sites several times a day to respond to 911 calls, in shorter, more frequent flights.

Source: NOPD Drone Flights, data.nola.gov · 742 flights from Jan 1 to Nov 5, 2026 · Data pulled Nov 18, 2026

Flights per week, by purpose

Each bar is one week. Event spikes (Mardi Gras, French Quarter Fest, Essence) dominated until October, when dispatch flights took over. Hover over a bar for its breakdown.

About this data

  • NOPD publishes one row per flight. The dataset description links to NOPD's drone policy and Skydio's public flight dashboard.
  • Takeoff times in the dataset appear to be in UTC. For example, the first "January 1" flights are logged at 6–7 a.m., which is just after midnight local time on New Year's. We converted all times to Central time.
  • Purposes come from a free-text field with many spellings. We grouped them into the categories above, for example combining "Bourbon St barricade check" variants into "Barricade & patrol checks."
  • Launch points are where the drone took off, not where it flew. Five flights have no location.
