Calls to NOPD in 2026

Every 911 call, report and officer-initiated stop gets logged as a "call for service." This page covers what people call about, when and where, how long it takes an officer to arrive, and how 2026 compares with every year since 2014.

About this data

  • The Orleans Parish Communication District (911) publishes one row per incident. NOPD can reclassify a call's type for up to 36 hours.
  • Calls "started by officers" are marked self-initiated in the data. Everything else counts as from the public, including alarms and calls from other agencies.
  • Response times exclude calls with no recorded arrival and arrivals over 24 hours. Priority codes come from the data's priority field. NOPD treats priority 2 as emergency and priority 1 as non-emergency.
  • We assigned neighborhoods and districts from each call's mapped point, using the City's Neighborhood Statistical Area boundaries.
  • The 2014–2025 history comes from the City's separate yearly Calls for Service datasets.
